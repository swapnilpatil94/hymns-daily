## Full description (2335 chars)
Hymns Daily is a free hymn book offline for anyone who loves church hymns in English. Open the app, tap a hymn, and it plays with organ or piano music, with no internet connection, no sign-up and no special permissions.

HYMNS WITH AUDIO, EVERY ONE OF THEM
Every one of the 245 classic hymns plays with full harmony. Choose a church organ or piano sound, slow the tempo for learning or singing along, or move the key up or down by three semitones to suit your voice. Prefer to read music? Show the sheet music for any hymn with one tap.

HYMN OF THE DAY
Each morning the Today screen offers a new hymn of the day and a verse of the day. Advent and Christmas bring carols, and the Easter season brings Easter hymns. Keep your streak going with an optional daily reminder at the time you choose.

A HYMNAL SONG BOOK BUILT FOR READING
Large, clear lyrics with the refrain after every verse, and a text size slider that remembers your choice. Light, dark or system theme keeps evening devotions easy on the eyes. Share a hymn with a friend in one tap.

FIND ANY HYMN FAST
Search by title, first line, any words from the verses, or by hymn number from 1 to 245. Browse by category or topic, or jump through the A to Z index. Save favourites and return to recently viewed hymns. When the same hymn has more than one tune, you will see each tune listed.

CLASSIC CHRISTIAN HYMNS AND GOSPEL HYMNS
The collection draws on the Open Hymnal Project and includes Christmas, Easter, Advent, Lent, Pentecost, thanksgiving, evening and morning hymns, along with well-loved gospel hymns and worship songs for Sunday services, family prayer and quiet moments at home. All hymn texts and music are in the public domain.

SCRIPTURE
A daily verse from the World English Bible (public domain) sits beside the hymn of the day.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account. No tracking of your reading. No storage or microphone permissions. Everything works in airplane mode. The app is free and supported by ads, and a one-time purchase removes ads for good. You can restore it on any device that uses your Google account.

Perfect for church musicians, choir members, Sunday school teachers, families and anyone who wants a simple, fast hymn book on their phone.

Hymn texts and music: Open Hymnal Project (public domain). Scripture: World English Bible (public domain).
