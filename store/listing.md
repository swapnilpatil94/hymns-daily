## Full description (2174/4000)
Hymns Daily is a Christian hymn book you can carry everywhere. 245 classic hymns, every one with organ or piano music, full lyrics and sheet music. It all works offline, with no account and no sign-up.

♪ EVERY HYMN PLAYS WITH MUSIC
Tap Play and hear the hymn tune in full four-part harmony. Choose a church organ or a piano, slow the tempo to learn a new hymn, or raise or lower the key to suit your voice. It is a hymnal with audio for choir practice, Sunday school, family worship or singing alone.

☀ HYMN OF THE DAY AND VERSE OF THE DAY
Each morning brings a new hymn of the day and a Bible verse of the day. Christmas carols arrive in Advent, Easter hymns in Holy Week. Turn on a gentle daily reminder to keep your devotional streak.

✝ CLASSIC HYMNS YOU KNOW AND LOVE
Amazing Grace, It Is Well With My Soul, Holy, Holy, Holy, Be Thou My Vision, Abide With Me, Rock of Ages, What a Friend We Have in Jesus, Blessed Assurance, Silent Night, Joy to the World and many more traditional church hymns and gospel hymns.

📖 HYMN LYRICS MADE FOR READING
Large, clear words with the refrain after every verse. Adjust the text size, and switch between a warm parchment day theme and a candlelight night theme for evening prayer.

🔍 FIND ANY HYMN FAST
Search by title, first line or any words from the lyrics, or type a hymn number. Browse by season and topic: Advent, Christmas, Epiphany, Lent, Easter, Pentecost, praise, trust, thanksgiving, evening and the Lord's Supper. Save favourites and return to recent hymns.

✈ 100% OFFLINE HYMNS
The hymns, music and sheet music are stored on your phone. Use the app in church, on a plane, or anywhere without internet or mobile data.

🔒 PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
No account. No storage, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Your favourites stay on your device. The app is free with ads, and a single one-time purchase removes ads forever.

Hymns Daily is for church musicians, choir members, worship leaders, Sunday school teachers, families and anyone who loves traditional Christian hymns.

All hymn texts and tunes are in the public domain, from the Open Hymnal Project. Scripture is from the World English Bible (public domain).
