Privacy Policy: Hymns Daily

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Hymns Daily ("the app") is designed to work offline and to collect as little as possible.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics SDK and no backend. Your favourites, recently viewed hymns, settings, reminder time and reading streak are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app first asks for your consent through Google's consent form. With your consent, Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is owned on your account.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and ads are not child-directed.

Content sources

Hymn texts and music: Open Hymnal Project (public domain). Scripture: World English Bible (public domain).
